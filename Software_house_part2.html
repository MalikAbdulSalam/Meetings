Business Launch Plan

Software House Investment & Client Acquisition

A practical starting plan for building a small AI, Computer Vision, Robotics, Automation and Software Development company.

1. Executive Summary

Recommended starting investment: approximately $200–$400 per month for the first 2–3 months.

A new software house does not need a large office, expensive servers, or a large advertising budget at the beginning.

The initial priority should be client acquisition, portfolio development, professional business infrastructure and reliable development equipment.

Core principle: Start lean, acquire clients, generate revenue, then reinvest the revenue into the company.

$20–100/mo

Fiverr

$0–100/mo

Build specialized gigs around specific services instead of offering generic software development.

PeoplePerHour

$0–50/mo

Treat this as a secondary source and measure whether it produces qualified leads.

Guru

$0–50/mo

Use as an additional lead source without making it a major marketing expense.

LinkedIn

Low initial cost

Build long-term relationships with companies, founders, CTOs and industrial businesses.

10. Customer Acquisition Cost

Track how much you spend to acquire each client.

Example:

Marketing and platform spending = $300
Clients acquired = 2

$150 customer acquisition cost per client.

If those two clients generate several thousand dollars in revenue, the acquisition cost is reasonable.

12. Infrastructure

A new software house does not need expensive physical infrastructure. Start with professional digital infrastructure.

Essential

  • Professional domain
  • Company email
  • Website
  • GitHub organization
  • Cloud storage
  • Project management
  • Reliable internet
  • Developer computers
  • Backup system

Not Required Initially

  • Large office
  • Dedicated server room
  • Expensive physical GPU cluster
  • Large IT department
  • Large full-time development team
For AI workloads, use cloud GPU resources when required instead of purchasing an expensive GPU server before the business needs it.
